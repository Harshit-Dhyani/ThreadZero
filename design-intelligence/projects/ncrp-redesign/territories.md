## A — Civic Editorial

Composition: asymmetric two-column civic opening. Large plain headline and urgent official-action band on the left; original documentary still life of a phone, bank message printout, notebook, and evidence envelope on the right. The lower edge reveals the first task-routing transition.

Typography: sober editorial serif for display paired with a highly legible humanist sans.

Palette: warm paper, charcoal, deep oxblood action, muted indigo structure.

Geometry: broad margins, fine rules, one crisp action, restrained radius.

Motion hypothesis: editorial reveals only; no essential motion.

Do not include: seals, flags, uniforms, government logos, victims, fake proof, dark cyber imagery, gradients, or fine embedded text.

## B — Evidence Atlas

Composition: light incident chronology runs diagonally through the viewport. Three tactile evidence objects attach to numbered events while the urgent official-action card and start action remain plainly visible. It should feel like an evidence table, not a dashboard.

Typography: compact grotesk for labels with a sturdy serif for the core message.

Palette: chalk, graphite, saffron-red urgency, mineral blue, muted evidence tags.

Geometry: ruled lines, square evidence tabs, small numbered anchors, minimal cards.

Motion hypothesis: connectors draw only when motion is permitted.

Do not include: maps, charts, network nodes, threat scores, surveillance motifs, glowing lines, fake metrics, or tiny UI copy.

## C — Guided Relief

Composition: a calm sequential entry with one clear decision, a visible three-step service path, and a warm original still life of hands sorting neutral evidence objects. Official-action guidance remains persistent and stronger than the concept action.

Typography: large rounded grotesk with a quiet serif accent for reassurance.

Palette: warm cream, ink, clay red urgency, sage guidance, blue-violet focus.

Geometry: generous touch targets, soft but not pill-heavy containers, a single primary action.

Motion hypothesis: a subtle step transition with reduced-motion fallback.

Do not include: testimonials, portraits as victims, AI assistants, mascots, gradients, official marks, or promise language.

## Selection rubric

- Immediate official-action clarity: 25
- Independent-concept honesty: 20
- Stress reduction and task confidence: 20
- Evidence/chronology differentiation: 15
- Responsive plausibility: 10
- Originality without copied identity: 10

The rubric informs critique but does not replace human selection.
