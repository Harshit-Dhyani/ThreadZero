## Synthesis

The strongest design opportunity is not a more dramatic cyber aesthetic. It is a composed public-service experience that makes urgency, evidence, chronology, and recovery understandable at a glance.

The homepage opening must answer four questions without scrolling:

1. Is this official?
2. What should I do right now?
3. What can this concept help me prepare?
4. What will happen to my information?

The reporting flow should make evidence feel manageable. Ready, Missing, and Optional states should describe preparation rather than judge the person reporting.

## Adopt

- Persistent official-action strip for 1930 and cybercrime.gov.in.
- Structural independent-concept disclaimer.
- One dominant action per decision state.
- Incident-first routing before detailed fields.
- Evidence grouped into an understandable incident thread.
- Preflight explanation and honest after-submission expectations.
- Editorial media used to orient, never to impersonate authority.
- Original overhead evidence, hands-and-device, and wide documentary crops derived from the audited image roles.
- The approved user reference's wide split, navy headline hierarchy, pale technical texture, and four-action rhythm without its official identity.

## Reject

- Government emblems, copied identities, flags as decoration, or implied official status.
- Dark cyber dashboards, hacker imagery, threat maps, neon networks, and surveillance metaphors.
- Generic blue-purple gradients, AI orbs, robots, card spam, fake metrics, testimonials, client logos, and recovery promises.
- Rasterized form text, buttons, statuses, or instructions in production.
- Third-party reference files in the public site.
- Embedded screenshots or rasterized form content; device screens in generated imagery remain abstract.

## Three visual territories

### A. Civic Editorial

Calm institutional clarity with asymmetrical editorial scale. Urgent guidance is a strong typographic interruption, paired with an original documentary-style evidence still life.

### B. Evidence Atlas

Evidence and chronology form the visual spine. A light, tactile incident map explains how transactions, messages, and actions become a coherent report without resembling a dashboard.

### C. Guided Relief

A humane sequential entry that reduces the first decision to a clear choice. Warm material photography and visible steps communicate support without testimonials or personas.

No territory is approved. Responsive HTML, the design constitution, section boards, production assets, and the full product build remain behind the human selection gate.
