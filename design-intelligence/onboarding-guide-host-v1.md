Full body, three-quarter view, warm reassuring expression, neat salt-and-pepper hair and moustache, navy and civic-blue casual jacket over a cream shirt, simple neutral trousers and shoes. Use tasteful stylized proportions, soft studio lighting, restrained materials, dark-navy and civic-blue accents, and a clean transparent background. No crown, king, joker, mascot imitation, government uniform, seal, shield, lock, logo, text, labels, controls, brand identity, or copyrighted character reference. The character must remain supplemental and contain no readable information.
