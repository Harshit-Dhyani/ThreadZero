# Pattern library

## Trust before persuasion

- State that this is an independent concept and uses synthetic data.
- Keep 1930 and cybercrime.gov.in visible as the real-world route.
- Say explicitly that no call or report is made.

## Urgency before onboarding

- Immediate financial-fraud action is visually separate from preparing a report.
- Do not bury urgent instructions in a general hero or FAQ.
- Use high contrast and literal verbs, not alarmist imagery.

## Incident before fields

- Ask what happened before requesting detailed evidence.
- Keep choices bounded and mutually understandable.
- Explain why each requested detail helps.

## Evidence as preparation

- Show Ready, Missing, and Optional.
- Missing evidence produces guidance, never a blocked route.
- Group evidence by incident event so chronology is understandable.

## Process visibility

- Communicate current position, next action, and what remains.
- Preserve backward navigation without losing synthetic session state.
- Review should summarize facts and uncertainties, not manufacture confidence.

## Editorial illustration

- Media supports orientation or explanation.
- Prefer coordinated two-dimensional civic illustration with believable people only when they clarify a task.
- Never rely on stock victims, hacker silhouettes, police imagery, maps, dashboards, or official symbols.
- Do not ship photography.
- HTML owns every word, form control, status, and interactive affordance.

## Landing-page rhythm

Each of the four sections should answer one group of related questions:

1. Where do I start, and what must I do urgently?
2. Which service route applies?
3. How do scattered records become an understandable evidence thread?
4. Where do I learn, get help, and reach the official destination?

## Anti-patterns

- Standalone marketing hero that does not prove the reporting service.
- Repeated generic cards with no hierarchy.
- Fake trust markers, proof, numbers, recovery claims, or testimonials.
- Raster text inside production imagery.
- A visual metaphor that becomes the interaction model.
